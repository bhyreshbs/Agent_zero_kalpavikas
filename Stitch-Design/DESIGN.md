## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes high-echelon state espionage dossiers with rare luxury editorial journals. Created for elite operatives, strategic directors, and high-clearance liaisons, the interface delivers severe operational gravity balanced with the tactile sophistication of bespoke archival print.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** and **Tactile/Editorial Skeuomorphism**:
- **Atmospheric Restraint:** Heavy, intentional dark space that avoids generic video game tropes, electric neons, and cybernetic glows. The tone is discreet, clandestine, and expensive.
- **Micro-Precision Engineering:** Clean hairline divisions, archival marginalia, serial stamps, and calibrated telemetry overlays that reflect physical briefing folders and encrypted station manifests.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Quiet authority, intellectual tension, absolute confidentiality, and tactical prestige.

## Colors

The palette relies on a warm-dark base with high-value tactile accents:

- **Neutral Canvas (`#0F0E0D` - Charcoal Slate):** The singular substrate for all displays, evoking matte obsidian, heavy archival leather, and blackout tactical rooms.
- **Surface Step-ups:**
  - Base Background: `#0F0E0D`
  - Dossier Card Surface: `#161513`
  - Nested Panel / Data Chamber: `#1C1A18`
  - Field Inspection Lift: `#23211E`
- **Primary Accent (`#D4A853` - Rich Amber-Gold):** Reserved for high-value clearance markers, seal debossing, critical focal points, status approvals, and fine linear accents.
- **Editorial Text (`#EDE8DF` - Warm Ivory):** High-contrast, glare-free readability for classified briefs, transcripts, and primary subject titles.
- **Secondary Index (`#7A7267` - Muted Stone):** Used for grid indexing, categorical legends, coordinate markers, and non-actionable structure.
- **Operational Status Sub-accents:**
  - Redaction / Threat Vector: `#9E382B` (Muted Crimson, strictly desaturated)
  - Station Surveillance / Verified: `#5E7862` (Deep Forest Olive, muted)

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional cadence between cinematic literature and technical instrumentation:

- **Playfair Display (Prestige Headlines, Subject Identifiers, Narrative Transcripts):** Evokes mid-century intelligence publications and high-end monographs. It introduces literary gravitas to case files and operative debriefs.
- **Space Grotesk (Navigation, Interactive UI, Control Nodes):** Modernist, calculated, and high-readability sans-serif for functional decisions, filters, and system-level actions. Always rendered with slight uppercase tracking for discipline.
- **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry, Coordinates, Redaction Stamps, Metadata):** Clean, unyielding machine-read data for cryptanalysis, frequency monitors, clearance levels, and location logs.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a strict **12-column architectural grid** evocative of archival ledger paper and intelligence floorplans.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 3.5rem outer canvas margins, 1.5rem gutters. Content conforms strictly to vertical rules. Telemetry rails occupy 2 columns; operative dossiers utilize 7 columns; situational metadata fills the final 3 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 2rem margins, 1rem gutters. Telemetry rails collapse into upper utility bars.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 1rem margins, 0.75rem gutters. Grid columns collapse into sequential dossier cards with accordion-style technical disclosures.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** 8px base grid. Sections are segmented by full-width 1px lines rather than blank vertical drop space, mimicking folding case folios.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects neon drop-shadows, blurred volumetric glows, and float animations in favor of planar, tactical, and tactile depth:

- **Tonal Stratification:** Hierarchy is established through distinct surface tiers (`#0F0E0D` -> `#161513` -> `#1C1A18`), allowing cards and sub-dossiers to feel mechanically inset or layered like physical paper sheets.
- **Ultra-Fine Structural Lines:** Panels, dividers, and framing elements use 1px solid borders (`rgba(212, 168, 83, 0.18)` for ambient frames, and `#D4A853` for active focus frames).
- **Subtle Vignetting & Grain:** Canvas edges feature an ultra-soft radial gradient falloff: `radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 30%, transparent 60%, rgba(5, 5, 5, 0.85) 100%)`. A micro-grain texture overlay (2% opacity noise) simulates archival dossier stock.
- **Optical Inset Shadowing:** Sunken data wells and code inspection fields use an inner shadow (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`) to visually recede into the charcoal slate surface.

## Shapes

The shape architecture is defined by **Absolute Sharpness (`roundedness: 0`)**.

- Every perimeter, button, input field, chip, and card terminates in strict 90-degree corners.
- Micro-cut corners (45-degree chamfers measuring 6px) are permitted exclusively for top-level dossier identifier tabs and mission clearance badges to simulate military-issue index cards.
- Dividers and indicator pips use crisp geometric squares and unyielding vertical/horizontal vectors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Operational Button:** Solid Amber-Gold (`#D4A853`) surface with Charcoal Slate (`#0F0E0D`) text in Space Grotesk Bold, fully uppercase. Zero corner radius. On hover, background shifts to Warm Ivory (`#EDE8DF`) with crisp transitions.
- **Secondary Dossier Button:** Ghost treatment with a 1px border in `rgba(212, 168, 83, 0.4)` and Warm Ivory (`#EDE8DF`) text. Hover elevates border to `#D4A853` and introduces a subtle inner tint of `rgba(212, 168, 83, 0.05)`.
- **Tertiary Utility Button:** Frameless, text-only in Space Grotesk (`#7A7267`), shifting to `#EDE8DF` with an amber tracking pip (`▪`) prepended upon hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Clearance Level Badge:** JetBrains Mono uppercase, bordered with 1px solid `#D4A853`, background `rgba(212, 168, 83, 0.08)`, text in `#D4A853`.
- **Classification Stamp:** Rotated or inset badge reading `TOP SECRET // ZERO` in desaturated crimson (`#9E382B`) with a distressed 1px enclosing box.

### Lists & Manifests
- Bordered modular rows. Each item is separated by a 1px border (`rgba(122, 114, 103, 0.2)`).
- Metadata coordinates (timestamp, channel) aligned left in JetBrains Mono (`#7A7267`), primary subject names centered in Playfair Display (`#EDE8DF`), status indicator aligned right.
- Row selection replaces the left border with an active 2px solid `#D4A853` vertical indicator.

### Checkboxes & Controls
- **Checkboxes:** Exact 14px x 14px squares with a 1px `#7A7267` border. Checked state renders a solid `#D4A853` square inner fill inset by 2px (no checkmark icon; strictly geometric mechanical fill).
- **Radio Buttons:** Exact 14px x 14px diamond (rotated square), unfilled on idle, filled with Amber-Gold diamond when armed.

### Input Fields
- **Tactical Field Entry:** Flat `#161513` surface, bottom-only 1px border in `#7A7267`. Active state transitions bottom line to 1px `#D4A853` and illuminates a blinking square cursor. Placeholder text in JetBrains Mono with 40% opacity. Label sits pinned above the field in Space Grotesk uppercase.

### Dossier Cards
- Background: `#161513` with 1px border in `rgba(212, 168, 83, 0.15)`.
- Header: Split container featuring operative codename in Playfair Display on the left and station coordinates / clearance rank in JetBrains Mono on the right.
- Separator: 1px hairline amber-tinted rule spanning the card width.
- Footer: Technical metadata readout and action anchors.

### Redaction Blocks
- Text elements marked classified are covered by an opaque rectangular block (`#0F0E0D` or `#1C1A18`) bordered with an ultra-thin 1px line in `#7A7267`, revealing the contents only upon authorized clearance hover.
